Coal Proximate–Ultimate Predictor

Convert a proximate analysis (moisture, volatile matter, ash, fixed carbon) into an ultimate analysis (carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen, sulphur, oxygen), or the other way round. Works with US (ASTM, as-received) and Indian (IS 1350, air-dried) reporting, lignite to anthracite.

How it works

  1. Strip out moisture and ash. The composition of the organic coal substance depends on rank, which is the same physics in Indian and US coals. Working on a dry, ash-free basis also makes the method independent of the reporting basis.
  2. Neural-network ensemble. Several networks read VMdaf, ash/organic matter and moisture/organic matter, and predict C, H, N and S on a dry, ash-free basis.
  3. Oxygen by difference, then conversion back to your basis, so that the sum closes exactly.
  4. Reverse direction. C + H + N + O + S gives the organic matter, so moisture + ash = 100 − (C + H + N + O + S) exactly. One network ensemble predicts VMdaf from the dry, ash-free elemental composition; a second estimates the moisture/ash split, which becomes exact if you enter the measured moisture.
Org = 100 − M − A VM_daf = 100 · VM / Org X = X_daf · Org / 100 (X = C, H, N, S) O = Org − C − H − N − S
